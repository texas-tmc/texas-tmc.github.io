About the Conference

The Texas Monetary Conference is an annual gathering of researchers in macroeconomics and monetary economics. Established in 1993, it is hosted on a rotating basis by six Texas institutions: the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas, Rice University, Southern Methodist University, Texas A&M University, the University of Houston, and the University of Texas at Austin.

The format has stayed essentially unchanged since the first meeting: first-rate macro and monetary economists from anywhere present their research, with discussants from the Texas institutions.
