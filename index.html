About the Conference

The Texas Monetary Conference brings together leading macro and monetary economists from anywhere, with discussants from the major Texas institutions. It started in 1993 at the University of Texas, with support from the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas, and has been held every year since, rotating among its host institutions.

The format has stayed essentially unchanged: invited papers from first-rate researchers, each followed by a prepared discussion from Texas faculty and an open discussion.

Participating Institutions

  • University of Texas at Austin
  • Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas
  • Texas A&M University
  • Rice University
  • Southern Methodist University
  • University of Houston
